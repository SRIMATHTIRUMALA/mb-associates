PM Surya Ghar: Muft Bijli Yojana Central DBT Slabs

Under the flagship PM Surya Ghar: Muft Bijli Yojana, financial assistance and subsidy eligibility are subject to DISCOM technical feasibility, load sanction, and official PM Surya Ghar National Portal guidelines. Subsidies are disbursed directly by the Government via Direct Benefit Transfer (DBT) upon net-meter inspection and are not guaranteed preset amounts.

Clear Answers

Frequently Asked Questions

Learn how our project assistance and vendor coordination structure protects your solar investment.

What is MB Associates' role versus a traditional solar installer? expand_more
MB Associates is an independent solar project assistance and coordination partner—we represent YOU. Traditional installers manufacture or warehouse specific brands and execute manual installations. We evaluate your actual technical requirements objectively, guide subsidy and bank paperwork, assign verified nearby installers, and monitor project milestones to make sure standards are adhered to without conflict of interest.
How does MB Associates select and verify local solar vendors? expand_more
We vet contractors for valid statutory credentials, active DISCOM empanelment, valid electrical supervisor licenses, and a verifiable track record in their pin code. Furthermore, vendors must agree to adhere to Tier-1 ALMM modules and BIS certified inverters rather than cheaper non-certified alternatives.
How does the PM Surya Ghar subsidy disbursement work? expand_more
Under the Central National Portal for Rooftop Solar, central subsidy assistance is paid via Direct Benefit Transfer (DBT) directly into the homeowner's verified bank account. Eligibility is strictly subject to DISCOM technical feasibility, single/three-phase sanctioned load, and PM Surya Ghar national portal guidelines. This occurs after physical installation, DISCOM net-meter inspection, and online commissioning report approval. MB Associates guides you through every step of this upload process without promising fixed figures.
Are solar bank loans or subsidy amounts guaranteed?expand_more
No, and anyone promising guaranteed loans is misleading you. Collateral-free solar loans offered through public/private financial institutions depend strictly on the applicant's credit score (CIBIL), banking history, and debt servicing capability. MB Associates assists you with structuring necessary quotations, system generation estimates, and compliant paperwork to maximize approval probability.
What happens after the solar panels are physically mounted on the roof? expand_more
Mounting the panels is only one milestone. Next, your local electricity distribution company (DISCOM) must inspect the wiring and earthing, supply and seal the bi-directional net meter, and activate the net-metering tariff schedule. MB Associates stays actively engaged through meter synchronization and initial bill verification.
