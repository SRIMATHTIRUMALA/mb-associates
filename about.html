Transparent Corporate Disclaimers & Scope of EPC

We value clear client communication without misleading energy yield or subsidy guarantees.

1. Independent EPC Status & Subsidies

MB Associates coordinates solar projects with nearby verified vendors and assists with loan applications, subsidy guidance, documentation, installation coordination and post-installation support. Financial subsidies under the PM Surya Ghar: Muft Bijli Yojana or state schemes are disbursed directly by the Government of India and local DISCOMs to the consumer's bank account. While we guide and facilitate all portal filings with zero commission deduction, sanction speed is subject to statutory verification queues and local power utility transformer capacity.

2. Empirical Generation Yield Assumptions

Quoted energy generation figures (kWh/kWp/year) represent standard solar irradiance conditions modeled via PVSyst or MNRE regional irradiance databases. Actual kilowatt-hour harvest varies based on seasonal atmospheric aerosol density, local soiling frequency, ambient rooftop temperatures, and periodic module cleaning regimens executed by the asset owner.

Government Compliance & Subsidy Guidelines Disclaimer:

Solar rooftop subsidies are governed by the Ministry of New and Renewable Energy (MNRE), National Portal for Rooftop Solar, and respective state electricity distribution companies (DISCOMs) under the PM Surya Ghar: Muft Bijli Yojana. Actual subsidy sanction and disbursement depend on applicant eligibility, roof stability approval, sanctioned load permissions, and DISCOM feeder availability. MB Associates adheres strictly to ALMM (Approved List of Models and Manufacturers) approved solar PV modules and BIS-certified inverters.
