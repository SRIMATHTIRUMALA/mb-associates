Government Compliance & Subsidy Guidelines Disclaimer:

Solar rooftop subsidies are governed by the Ministry of New and Renewable Energy (MNRE), National Portal for Rooftop Solar, and respective state electricity distribution companies (DISCOMs) under the PM Surya Ghar: Muft Bijli Yojana. Actual subsidy sanction and disbursement depend on applicant eligibility, roof stability approval, sanctioned load permissions, and DISCOM feeder availability. MB Associates adheres strictly to ALMM (Approved List of Models and Manufacturers) approved solar PV modules and BIS-certified inverters.
