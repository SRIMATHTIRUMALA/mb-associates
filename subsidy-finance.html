End-To-End Procedural Flow

The 4-Step Direct Benefit Transfer Roadmap

Under current guidelines, subsidies are not deducted up-front by vendors. Instead, they are directly deposited into your bank account by the government following net-meter integration.

1

National Portal Registration

Consumer registers on pmsuryaghar.gov.in using State DISCOM consumer electricity account number, mobile number, and Aadhaar-linked credentials.

app_registration Consumer Portal Account Created
2

DISCOM Feasibility Approval

The local electricity utility analyzes local distribution transformer (DT) load capacity. Once feasibility is granted, official sanction is issued on the portal.

verified Technical Sanction Issued
3

Turnkey EPC & Work Report

MB Associates installs BIS-standard, ALMM-compliant PV panels and grid inverter. Work completion report and geo-tagged photos are submitted to DISCOM.

solar_power ALMM & CEIG Standards Verified
4

Net-Meter & DBT Release

DISCOM installs the bi-directional net meter and generates the Commissioning Certificate. The central DBT amount is credited directly to your bank account via PFMS.

payments DBT Direct Credit in ~30 Days

Document Readiness Checklist for Subsidy & Net Metering

Avoid application rejection or delayed DBT disbursals on the National Rooftop Solar portal by ensuring your documentation matches your electricity utility registration details exactly.

Name Mismatch Advisory:

The name on your DISCOM electricity bill must match the name on your Aadhaar card and bank passbook. If there is a variation, update your electricity bill name with your DISCOM prior to portal submission.

Solar rooftop subsidies are governed exclusively by the Ministry of New and Renewable Energy (MNRE), National Portal for Rooftop Solar, and respective state electricity distribution companies (DISCOMs) under PM Surya Ghar: Muft Bijli Yojana. Actual subsidy release and green financing are eligibility-dependent, subject to DISCOM technical feasibility, structural roof approval, sanctioned load limits, and bank underwriting criteria. MB Associates coordinates solar projects with nearby verified vendors and assists with loan applications, subsidy guidance, documentation, installation coordination and post-installation support adhering strictly to ALMM-approved solar modules and BIS-certified inverters.
